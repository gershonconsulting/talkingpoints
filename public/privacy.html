Talking Points

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Talking Points (talkingpoints.gershon.ai) is a service of Gershon Consulting LLC, a Delaware company ("we", "us"). It is offered to clients of Gershon Consulting to prepare the first conversation with leads from their US campaign. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • LinkedIn sign-in: when you sign in with LinkedIn we receive your name, profile photo, email address and LinkedIn member identifier (scopes openid, profile, email). We use them to identify you and link you to your company's account.
  • Google Calendar (optional): if an account manager connects Google Calendar, we receive read-only access to calendar events and the calendar list (scope calendar.readonly), plus the account's email address. We use it only to find upcoming meetings with leads and prepare talking points for them. We never create, change or delete events.
  • Streak (optional): if you add a Streak API key, we read the pipeline you choose to see which leads were added. The key is stored encrypted.
  • Lead and profile information: public professional information about leads in your pipeline and about you and your team (employers, schools, locations, public posts), and company information from public registries, used to produce the briefs.
  • Usage data: basic technical logs (time of request, pages used) kept for security and troubleshooting.

How we use it

Only to provide Talking Points to you: signing you in, building lead briefs, matching each lead with the best-placed person on your team, and emailing you those briefs. We do not sell personal data, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to train general-purpose AI models.

Google user data

Talking Points' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google Calendar data is used only to prepare meeting talking points shown to your own account; it is not transferred to others except as needed to provide this feature, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you. No human reads your calendar data unless you ask us to for support, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.

Service providers

We use Cloudflare (hosting and database), Resend (email delivery) and AI processing providers to generate the briefs. They process data on our behalf and only to run the service.

Storage and security

Data is stored with Cloudflare. Access tokens and API keys are encrypted at rest. Access to the service is by invitation and limited to people in your company's account.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your company uses Talking Points. You can disconnect Google Calendar or Streak at any time in Settings, which deletes the stored token or key. You can also revoke access from your Google account or LinkedIn settings. To have your account and data deleted, email us and we will do it within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to its use, by emailing olivier@gershonconsulting.com.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell account users by email.
