# Talking Points — versions

Shown in the app sidebar, the Admin page and the home page footer. Bump on every release.

- **v1.11.1** (2026-11-13) — Fix: restore the email subject Olivier set on Nov 3, lost in later merges, without “[21]”: “Talking Points by Gershon.ai : Client - Lead company - Lead - Date” (client name without #number or (person)). - **v1.11.0** (2026-11-13) — Friday run widened: EVERY new lead in a client's Streak pipeline (created in the last 14 days, not only the newest 5) gets its researched talking point on Friday, never twice. Talking points now go to the client's whole team: Settings recipients + logins + the client's “send report” list in client.gershon.ai (v3:settings.clientEmails, by client number). - **v1.10.0** (2026-11-12) — New talking point on the fly: sidebar → “New talking point”, paste a company website or a professional email (personal mailboxes like Gmail are refused). The app reads the public website (home + about/team/contact pages) and Cloudflare Workers AI (Llama 3.3 70B) writes the talking point against the campaign's company, ICP and team: ICP fit verdict, snapshot, the company, the person (from the email), lead with this, call hooks, social channels linked from the site, questions, needs-your-attention. Labelled “Quick — AI from website/email”; saved per campaign (table quick_briefs) with Download PDF/HTML, Send by email (same client/internal rules and send log) and Delete. Never in the Leads list, the daily run or the Friday run. Limit 40 a day per campaign.
- **v1.9.4** (2026-11-11) — Friday report: once the Friday run is done, one email goes to report@gershonconsulting.com — “Talking Points Report — <date> (Friday run)” — with the number of talking points sent per client (from the send log), the leads, recipients, leads already sent earlier, research needed, and clients skipped (no ICP / no recipients). Sent once per Friday. Owner: GET /api/admin/friday-report?day=YYYY-MM-DD previews a Friday's report, POST resends it.

Gershon's own pipelines keep the “Scheduled” rule. Admin → “Friday talking points” shows, per client, the 5 newest leads: ✅ sent, 📨 goes out Friday, ❌ research needed. Owner-only POST /api/admin/briefs publishes a researched talking point from the browser (checks the lead is in that client's pipeline, never emails). Fix: the empty duplicate “#229 Altavia (Anne)” (slug altavia) no longer comes back — it was re-created from seed.sql on every cold start; Anne's real campaign is altavia-anne. Fix: app.js APP_VERSION matched to version.txt (a mismatch made open tabs reload in a loop).
