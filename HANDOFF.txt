Updated 2026-10-19

WHAT IT IS
Client-facing app for Gershon Consulting clients (pilot: Valos). Clients sign in with LinkedIn,
see the leads Gershon logs in their Streak "<Client> - LG" pipeline, and read a talking-points
brief per lead in the PEPperPRINT email format, plus a new "What you have in common" section
(best-matched teammate + overlaps).

STACK (same shape as Linalysis / radar)
- Static pages in public/ (Linalysis common.css, Gershon red #FE1B04)
- Cloudflare Pages Functions in functions/
- D1 database talkingpoints-db (schema.sql, seed.sql)
- Workers AI (binding AI) for the draft brief
- Resend for email (from talkingpoints@gershon.ai) — OFF until Olivier approves the format
- Deploy: push to main → .github/workflows/deploy.yml (creates D1 + Pages project, sets secrets,
  deploys, attaches talkingpoints.gershon.ai, registers Streak webhooks)

ROUTES
/                         home page
/login                    sign-in page (LinkedIn)
/app                      dashboard: Leads · My profile · Team
/api/auth/linkedin/start  → LinkedIn consent (scope openid profile email)
/api/auth/linkedin/callback   (registered in the LinkedIn app — Olivier confirmed)
/api/me                   GET profile/team, PUT own background
/api/leads                GET leads of the user's client pipeline (live from Streak) + brief status
/api/leads/:box           GET brief (+ rendered HTML), POST build a draft now
/api/streak/webhook?token=…   Streak BOX_CREATE → draft brief (+ email when enabled)
/api/briefs               POST researched brief JSON (Bearer INGEST_TOKEN) → status "ready"
/api/admin/webhooks       POST register Streak webhooks for all clients (Bearer INGEST_TOKEN)
/api/settings             GET/PUT client settings: own Streak API key (encrypted) + pipeline, recipient
                          emails, hours before meeting (default 48), Google Calendar
/api/google/start|callback    Connect Google Calendar (read-only), refresh token encrypted in D1
/api/meetings             GET upcoming meetings with prepared talking points
/api/cron/run             POST hourly (Bearer INGEST_TOKEN, .github/workflows/cron.yml):
                          new Streak leads → draft; meetings within lead_hours with an outside
                          attendee → brief (Streak match by email, else from the invite) → email once

BRIEF FORMAT
functions/_render.js documents the JSON and renders it. Sections, in order:
header · social grade · snapshot · what you have in common · lead with this · person · company ·
financials & ownership (only when public data exists) · previous conversation · LinkedIn & social
table · industry pulse · talking points & questions · next US events · needs your attention · sources

FINANCIALS (functions/_financials.js)
FR Pappers (PAPPERS_API_KEY) · UK Companies House (COMPANIES_HOUSE_API_KEY, free) ·
US listed companies SEC EDGAR (no key) · DE/IT/ES/BE/NL/CH: filled by the research step for now.

ACCESS
A LinkedIn sign-in is accepted when its email is in `members`, or its domain is in the client's
`email_domains`. Admins (is_admin=1) see the client view but are excluded from team matching.
Seeded: client "valos" (pipeline Valos - LG), admins oattia@gmail.com + olivier@gershonconsulting.com.

SETTINGS PAGE (Olivier, 2026-10-19)
Client provides CRM API access (Streak key), AI = Cloudflare Workers AI (no key), optional Google
Calendar to send talking points 48h before meetings, and the list of recipient emails.
